About SJACSOAA

The St. Joseph's Anglo-Chinese School Ontario Alumni Association (SJACSOAA) was founded by a group of SJACS graduates who had made Ontario, Canada their home. What began as informal gatherings of former classmates has grown into a warm and active community united by shared memories, a common school, and the spirit of “Ad Astra.” SJACSOAA brings together alumni from across the graduating years, providing a place to reconnect with old friends, celebrate milestones, and keep the SJACS tradition alive in Ontario. We organise annual dinners, festive gatherings, and reunions — and we welcome every SJACS graduate who finds themselves in this part of the world. Whether you graduated decades ago or more recently, if SJACS is part of your story, SJACSOAA is your home away from home.
